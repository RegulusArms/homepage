CUSTOM ARTWORK & ADULT CONTENT POLICY

Effective Date: 8/31/2026
Applies to: All custom UV-printed products (1911 grips, PMag panels, Picatinny rail covers, keychains, stickers, and any other customer-artwork product) ordered directly through Regulus Arms (regulusarms.com / Square Online).
Note on marketplaces: This policy governs direct orders only. GunBroker, eBay, Square and Facebook Shop each enforce their own (generally much stricter, often zero-tolerance) content rules for adult material, and those platform rules control any listing or order placed through them regardless of what this policy allows.

1. Purpose

Regulus Arms accepts customer-submitted artwork for custom printing. This policy defines what artwork we will and won't print, modeled on the content standards used by pixiv (a major illustration platform) for how it distinguishes acceptable artistic/mature content from prohibited content — with two additions specific to our business: a flat ban on non-consensual real nude imagery, and a rule that explicit full nudity is only accepted in drawn/illustrated form, never in real photographs.

2. Permitted Content

  • Illustrated / drawn artistic nudity (anime, digital painting, traditional art, comic/manga style, pin-up art, etc.) is permitted, including full nudity and explicit content, so long as it complies with Section 4 below.
  • Suggestive or mature-themed illustrated art (including swimwear, lingerie, romantic/intimate scenes, and similar material) is permitted, provided that it does not fall within any of the prohibited categories in Section 4.
  • Non-sexual artistic nudity (fine-art style nude figures, classical-style art, anatomical studies) is permitted without restriction.
  • Real photographs of people are accepted for non-nude, non-sexual custom designs (portraits, memorial pieces, unit photos, pet photos, etc.), subject to the rights/consent requirement in Section 3.

3. Real Photographs — Consent Requirement (Anti-"Revenge Porn" Rule)

  • Regulus Arms will not print any real photograph depicting nudity, partial nudity, or sexual content of an identifiable real person, under any circumstances — this applies even if the customer claims to have the subject's consent. Full nudity is accepted only in drawn/illustrated form (see Section 2).
  • For all other real-person photographs, the customer submitting the order must affirmatively certify at checkout that they own the rights to the image and have the legal right to use it, and that its use doesn't violate any third party's privacy or publicity rights.
  • Any order suspected of using an image of a real, identifiable person without their consent — including but not limited to intimate imagery shared without consent — will be canceled and refused, and Regulus Arms reserves the right to retain records of the submission in case a request for legal cooperation follows.

4. Absolutely Prohibited Content (No Exceptions, Any Medium)

The following will never be printed, in any style, drawn or photographic:

  • Any sexual or nude content involving minors, or content depicting what could reasonably be mistaken for a minor in a sexualized context.
  • Content depicting non-consensual sexual acts.
  • Content sexualizing a real, identifiable person (public figure or private individual) without their consent, including AI-generated or edited images placing a real person's likeness into nude or sexual content.
  • Bestiality or extreme sexual violence. (Clarification: this refers to sexual content involving real or feral animals. It does not include anthropomorphic ("furry") characters drawn with human-style anatomy and proportions — those are treated as original characters under Section 2, not as bestiality.)
  • Content that Regulus Arms reasonably believes is unlawful, legally obscene, or otherwise prohibited by applicable federal, state, or local law.

5. Order Requirements

  • Customers must confirm they are 18 or older to submit or order adult-themed custom artwork.
  • Customers must certify they have usage rights or are otherwise allowed to use any submitted artwork or photograph — whether through Creative Commons or another open license, verbal or written approval from the artist/rights holder, an artist's stated policy allowing personal use of their work, or because it is the customer's own original creation.
  • Regulus Arms reserves the right to refuse, cancel, or refund any order if, in our reasonable judgment, the submitted artwork may violate this policy, applicable law, the rights of another person, or the rules of a platform through which the order was placed. We may decline to provide detailed information regarding our decision where doing so could compromise customer privacy, legal obligations, or internal review procedures.
