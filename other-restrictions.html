OTHER PRINTING RESTRICTIONS

Applies to: All custom UV-printed products (1911 grips, pistol grips, PMag panels, Picatinny rail covers, keychains, stickers, and any other customer-artwork product) ordered directly through Regulus Arms.
See also: Adult Content Policy for rules on adult and nude artwork.

1. Law Enforcement Agency Logos & Badges

Regulus Arms will not print grips or any other product featuring the logo, badge, patch, seal, or other official insignia of any local, state, federal, or tribal law enforcement agency without written approval from that agency's chief, sheriff, director, commandant, or equivalent head of the agency.

  • This rule exists to prevent misuse of official agency logos and badges, including impersonation of officers or agencies.
  • Approval must be in writing, on agency letterhead or from an official agency email address, and must clearly identify the insignia to be printed and authorize its use on the requested product.
  • Approval from an individual officer, employee, or member who is not the head of the agency is not sufficient; approval must come from the head of the agency personally.
  • Regulus Arms may verify any approval directly with the agency before printing, and will cancel and refund any order where approval can't be verified.

2. Other Government Agency Logos & Insignia

The same rule applies to the logo, seal, badge, patch, or other official insignia of any other government agency or department — local, state, federal, or tribal — such as a city sewer or water department, roads and bridges / public works, parks department, fire department, or similar. Regulus Arms will not print these without written approval from that agency's director, manager, or equivalent head of the agency.

  • The approval requirements from Section 1 apply: written, on agency letterhead or from an official agency email address, identifying the insignia and the product, and coming from the head of the agency personally.
  • Regulus Arms may verify any approval directly with the agency and will cancel and refund any order where approval can't be verified.

3. Lookalikes, Official Titles & Wording

Regulus Arms will not print designs that imitate or could reasonably be mistaken for the insignia of a government agency, even if they aren't an exact copy, and will not print official titles or wording that present the product as agency-issued or the holder as an official.

  • Lookalike designs: near-copies, altered versions, or generic badges, stars, shields, and seals styled to look like law enforcement, military, or other government insignia.
  • Official titles and wording: agency names and titles such as "Police", "Sheriff", "State Trooper", "Federal Agent", "FBI", "ICE", "DEA", "ATF", "US Marshal", "Border Patrol", "Special Agent", or "Task Force" when used in a way that presents the item as official or the holder as an officer or agent.
  • Written approval from the agency head under Sections 1 and 2 covers the agency's actual insignia only and does not permit lookalikes or altered versions of it.
  • Regulus Arms decides in its reasonable judgment whether a design could be mistaken for official insignia, and may refuse or cancel and refund any order on that basis.

4. Corporate Logos & Trademarks

Regulus Arms will not print the trademarked logo, emblem, or insignia of any corporation or company without written approval from that company or its authorized representative.

  • This applies to firearm and accessory manufacturers, brands, sports teams, and any other business, and covers lookalike or altered versions of a company's marks.
  • Approval must be in writing, clearly identify the logo to be printed, and authorize its use on the requested product.
  • Regulus Arms may verify any approval directly with the company and will cancel and refund any order where approval can't be verified.
